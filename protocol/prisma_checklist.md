# PRISMA 2020 Checklist (minimal working copy)

Item 2: Abstract — Provide a structured summary of the review.
Item 3: Rationale — Describe the rationale for the review.
Item 4: Objectives — Provide an explicit statement of the objectives.
Item 5: Eligibility criteria — Specify inclusion and exclusion criteria.
Item 6: Information sources — Specify all databases and sources searched.
Item 7: Search strategy — Present the full search strategies.
Item 8: Selection process — State the process for selecting studies.
Item 9: Data collection process — Describe methods of data collection.
Item 10: Data items — List and define all variables/data items sought.
Item 11: Study risk of bias assessment — Specify the methods used.
Item 12: Effect measures — Specify the effect measures used.
Item 13: Synthesis methods — Describe methods to synthesize results.
Item 14: Reporting bias assessment — Describe assessment methods.
Item 15: Certainty assessment — Describe certainty of evidence approach.
Item 16: Study selection — Present results of the search and selection.
Item 17: Study characteristics — Cite characteristics of included studies.
Item 18: Risk of bias in studies — Present risk of bias assessments.
Item 19: Results of individual studies — Present summary statistics.
Item 20: Results of syntheses — Present meta-analysis / synthesis results.
Item 21: Reporting biases — Present assessments of reporting bias.
Item 22: Certainty of evidence — Present certainty for outcomes.
Item 23: Discussion — Interpret results in context of other evidence.
Item 24: Limitations — Discuss limitations of evidence and review process.
Item 25: Support — Describe sources of support/funding and role of sponsors.
Item 26: Competing interests — Declare any COI.
Item 27: Availability of data, code and other materials — State where available.
